# Prepared observation data

`observed-birds.json` is the server-read snapshot generated by
`npm run prepare:observations`. Each entry has one accepted species, its latest
usable observation date, and public coordinates. No observer names, notes or
location-precision fields are retained. The species count is derived from these
entries, not hard-coded.

Each bird also has prepared `media`: its English name, a medium iNaturalist
card photo and a local transparent WebP cutout path, up to four large
iNaturalist detail photos, a Wikipedia-derived
description where available, and one Xeno-canto recording. Photo and audio
credits, licenses, and source links are stored with the content. The homepage
does not query these services during browsing. `npm run prepare:media` can
enrich the current snapshot without downloading observations again.
The cutout images live in `public/bird-cutouts/`; the JSON stores their paths,
not image bytes. The original photo URL and credit remain in the JSON.

`norwegian-scope.geojson` is a saved geographic subset of:

Flanders Marine Institute (2024). Union of the ESRI Country shapefile and the
Exclusive Economic Zones (version 4). https://doi.org/10.14284/698
License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://www.marineregions.org/

Retrieved through `https://geo.vliz.be/geoserver/MarineRegions/wfs`, using:

- service=WFS, version=1.0.0, request=GetFeature
- typeName=MarineRegions:eez_land, outputFormat=application/json
- srsName=EPSG:4326
- cql_filter: territory1 IN ('Norway','Svalbard','Jan Mayen') OR territory2 IN ('Norway','Svalbard','Jan Mayen')

This includes mainland Norway/coastal islands, Svalbard/Bjørnøya, Jan Mayen,
their maritime zones, and shared regime polygons involving those territories.
It excludes other Norwegian dependencies, foreign land/waters, and the high seas
outside these polygons. This is a product boundary based on the published
version-4 dataset, not a claim about sovereignty or an official bird checklist.
Original geometries are retained; the change is the geographic subset selection.

Observation source: public Artskart API, https://artskart.artsdatabanken.no/publicapi/
Taxonomy source: Artsnavn/Nortaxa, https://nortaxa.artsdatabanken.no/
See `docs/monthly-refresh.md` for preparation, tie-breaking and refresh behavior.
